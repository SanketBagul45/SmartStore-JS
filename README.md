# 🛒 SmartStore JS

A simple, lightweight e-commerce store built with **HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript**. Add products, view their details, and manage a shopping cart — all powered by `localStorage`, no backend required.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [https://smartstore-js.netlify.app/](https://smartstore-js.netlify.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Add Products** — Add new products with an image URL, name, description, and price.
- **View Products** — Browse all added products in a responsive card grid.
- **View More** — Click "View More" to see full product details in a popup modal.
- **Add to Cart** — Add any product to your shopping cart with one click.
- **Cart Management** — View, remove items from your cart, and place an order.
- **Persistent Storage** — Products and cart data are saved in the browser's `localStorage`, so your data stays even after refreshing.

## 🖥️ Usage

1. **Add a Product** — On the Home page, fill in the product image URL, name, description, and price, then click **Add Product**.
2. **Browse Products** — All products appear as cards below the form.
3. **View More** — Click **View More** on any product card to see its full details in a popup.
4. **Add to Cart** — Click **Add To Cart** to add the product to your cart.
5. **Manage Cart** — Go to the **Add Cart** page to view cart items, remove them, or click **Buy Now** to place the order.
